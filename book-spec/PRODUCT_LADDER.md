# Book 1 Product Ladder

Commercial structure is a hypothesis to validate with real readers, not a promise of revenue or a fixed price list. The core ebook must stand on its own and must not hide essential safety instruction behind an upsell.

| Tier | Product | Includes | Release condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Essentials** | Ebook + reference pack | EPUB/PDF manuscript, glossary, dated official references, templates, checklists, errata/update page | Ebook is edited, sourced, technically reproduced, beginner-tested, and accessibility checked |
| **Builder** | Essentials + practical kit | Prompt library, starter repositories/milestone snapshots, worksheets, troubleshooting guide, project checklists | Starter code is reproducible; prompts are versioned; repositories contain no secrets; project acceptance checks are documented |
| **Professional** | Builder + guided practice | Video walkthroughs, structured capstone, optional human review, community/cohort, completion rubric | Real humans are available to deliver review/support; review scope, turnaround, privacy, and limitations are explicit |

## Rules for honest packaging

- Do not describe a certificate as a professional license or proof of job readiness.
- Do not promise “lifetime updates” unless the publisher can sustain them; state an update period or policy.
- Do not sell human capstone review until qualified reviewers, process, capacity, and data-handling rules exist.
- Do not market starter repositories as production-ready templates.
- Keep all core safety content in the book, regardless of tier.
- Validate pricing, refund terms, distribution fees, tax, platform AI-disclosure requirements, and demand with current primary sources and commercial research before launch.
- Consider purchasing power, local payment options, and bandwidth constraints in the target markets; do not assume one currency or free-tier access works everywhere.
